# Store listing copy

The App Store and Google Play text, written to the website's line: "The wine app built just for you."
Paste each block into its field. Limits are the stores' own; the counts were checked when this was written.

Rules it follows (the same as the site's): never "the first" or "the best" (Apple and Google reject claims
that can't be proved), no competitor named anywhere (keywords included), people rate wines and the app
gives a match, and "here to teach, not to sell" goes no further than what's true: Vinterest doesn't sell
wine, and shop links never change a match or the order of a list.

    The wine app built just for you.

## Before pasting

- The description avoids words that are spelled differently in the US and the UK, so one text serves
  every English listing. The prices in the Pro paragraph are the UK's: change them per country's listing.
- Pro's line says what the plans do today (`FREE_SCANS`, `FREE_GRAPE_CAP`, `FREE_REGION_CAP`, `PRO_ONLY`).
  Change it here when the plans change. Pro's scans are under a fair-use cap, so it says "more scans",
  never "unlimited".
- Screenshots carry the same line: the first one's caption should be the subtitle.
