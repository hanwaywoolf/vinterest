CAVIAR VANCOUVER FAQ

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT CAVIAR IN VANCOUVER

Discover where to find the best caviar in Vancouver, how to enjoy it, and why Social Corner offers one of the most approachable luxury caviar experiences in the city.

Where can I find the best caviar in Vancouver?

Social Corner is one of the top destinations for caviar in Vancouver, offering Baerii and Oscietra in a vibrant Yaletown setting at an accessible price point. It is a unique way to enjoy premium caviar in a social and modern dining atmosphere.

Is there an affordable way to try caviar in Vancouver?

Yes. Our Caviar Bump starts at $15, making it one of the most affordable caviar experiences in Vancouver without compromising quality, sourcing, or freshness.

What is a caviar bump and how is caviar served in Vancouver?

A caviar bump is a 5g serving enjoyed directly from the back of the hand. It is one of the purest ways to experience caviar, allowing the full flavor profile to shine without interference from garnishes.

What drinks pair best with caviar in Vancouver?

Champagne and dry sparkling wines are ideal pairings for caviar. They cleanse the palate and enhance the briny, buttery notes. At Social Corner, we offer curated pairings designed for a full caviar tasting experience in Vancouver.

Is your caviar in Vancouver sustainably sourced?

Yes. We work with ethical farms to ensure our caviar meets the highest standards of sustainability, freshness, and premium quality.

Where can I try a caviar tasting experience in Vancouver?

Social Corner offers curated pairings and shareable formats, making it one of the best places for a caviar tasting in Vancouver, whether you're a first-time guest or an experienced connoisseur.

Is caviar available for takeout in Vancouver or dine-in only?
