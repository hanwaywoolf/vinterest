## The basics

- **Name:** Social Corner. The logo reads "per se SOCIAL CORNER", and the site's title is "Per Se Social Corner". Owned by **Per Se Hospitality Group** (https://persehospitalitygroup.com/), which also runs Acquafarina (Italian, https://acquafarina.com/) and Ciclo Espresso (café and cocktail bar, https://cicloespressocafe.com/).
- **Two locations, one menu.** The food menu says "All dishes available at both locations"; three desserts are Yaletown only.
  - **Yaletown:** 891 Homer St, Vancouver, BC V6B 2W2. (604) 428-6111. info@socialcorner.com. Group bookings: yaletowngroupbookings@socialcorner.com
  - **Coal Harbour:** 1050 W Cordova St, Vancouver, BC V6E 2E9. (604) 336-8656. infoch@socialcorner.com. Group bookings: chgroupbookings@socialcorner.com
  - The site footer also gives info@persesocialcorner.com, 604.428.6111. Media & marketing: today@persehospitalitygroup.com / today@persesocialcorner.com
- **Opening hours** (Locations page, same for both): Mon–Thu 11 AM–11 PM; Fri–Sat 11 AM–12 AM; Sun 11 AM–11 PM. Brunch 11 AM–2:30 PM daily. Happy Hour daily 2:30–5:30 PM. Late Night Happy Hour 9 PM–close Sun–Thu (the home page and the Yaletown booking page add 10 PM–close Fri–Sat; the happy hour menu and Locations page list Sun–Thu only). The Yaletown booking page calls the 2:30–5:30 slot "Sweet Spot".
- **Cuisine and concept:** Italian and Spanish together: tapas, pastas, wood-fired ("800-Degree", "open-flame brick forno") pizza, paellas, steaks, plus a caviar programme (bumps from $15) and a well-known burger. Michelin Guide recommended. Cocktail-led bar with named bartender signatures.
- **Reservations:** OpenTable (Yaletown rid 991708). Online ordering via DoorDash. Gift cards are group-wide (Toast e-gift cards).
- **Events:** Yaletown full buyout 270 standing / 140 seated (170 with lounge), Café venue 45 standing / 36 seated, "The Stage" semi-private 24 seated. Coal Harbour full buyout 250 standing / 200 seated with patio, enclosed patio 125/90, outdoor patio 55/46, private dining room 10. Event menus: three course $75, four course $95 or $125 per person, or passed canapés and platters.
