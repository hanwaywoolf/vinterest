Scan

Scan a bottle. Know if it's for you.

Point your camera at any label or wine list. You get a match built from your own scores, and the reasons behind it.

  1. A match you can read as a score

    87% means we expect you to give it about an 87. It comes from every wine of that type you've scored, weighted by how alike each one is.

  2. Reasons for and against, and exactly why

    Syrah: you've scored two, averaging 90. Tannins: your 90+ reds lean softer, and this one doesn't. Tap “Why 87%?” to open the working, and the bottles you've tried that are most like it.

Learn about it

Learn about the wine, in nine cards.

One tap on “Learn about it” opens a deck written for this exact bottle: why it suits you, where it's from, how to taste it, what to say, and what it should cost.

  1. Start with your match

    The first card is the match and its reasons, in big type.

  2. Why you'll like it, and what to watch for

    A line on how this bottle fits your taste, and one practical tip, like decanting or serving temperature.

  3. Where it's from, and a story to tell

    The place, what makes the region's wines distinctive, the producer, and one surprising fact.

  4. How to taste it, and what to say

    Cues for body, tannins and acidity as you sip, Blind Call if you want to test your palate, and three lines to sound clued-in.

  5. What it should cost

    The shop price where you live, set against what you usually spend. It sits on its own card and never changes the match.

  6. Then score it

    The last card is where you give it a score.

9:41

Rate it

Taste it, then score it out of 100.

Tap a quick score or drag the slider. Your score is the most useful thing you can give Vinterest, and every one sharpens your next match.

  1. Slide to a score

    Drag the slider or tap 70, 80, 85, 90 or 95. The label follows: 90 to 95 is Outstanding, 80 to 89 Very good.

  2. It joins your WineDNA

    Save it and the bottle goes into My Wines and starts shaping your WineDNA and your next matches.

  3. Add what you noticed, if you like

    Say whether you'd buy it again, what you paid and how it felt against the label. Every answer is used: buy again feeds your shopping list, and the price sets your budget.

Keep learning

Then keep learning about the bottle.

The moment you score, Vinterest offers a few things to read and quiz yourself on, all picked for the wine you just had. A few minutes each.

  1. Quizzes on the bottle

    The basics for its type, a quiz on where it's from, and one on its grape. Each scan opens its own.

WineDNA

Every bottle builds your WineDNA.

Your scores add up to a profile of the grapes, regions, styles and prices you love. It remembers everything you've tried, so you don't have to.

  1. A profile for each kind of wine

    Reds, whites, rosé and sparkling each get their own read. This drinker's reds come out Classic & Balanced.

  2. What you love, in numbers

    Body, tannins and acidity across the wines you scored 90 or more, next to the rest. You see what you keep reaching for.

  3. Grapes, regions and value

    Your top grape, your best-scoring region, and what you pay for the wines you rate highest.

  4. Where to look next

    Suggestions built from what you already love, including the steps just outside your comfort zone.

Ask Vinny

The right wine, wherever you're buying.

In the shop, at the restaurant or browsing online. Got a quick question? Ask Vinny. His answers come from your WineDNA, not from a list of bestsellers.

  1. Ask it the way you'd say it

    "Steak tonight. What should I look for?" No wine vocabulary needed.

  2. An answer about you

    Vinny knows your best-scoring bottles, what you usually spend and what didn't work, and he uses them.

  3. Short, with somewhere to go next

    Two or three sentences you can act on in the aisle, and a link to learn more when there's something worth reading.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free?

You can start for free. Some features, like scanning whole wine lists and the Mastery map, are part of Vinterest Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wines and scores live on your phone. An account is optional, and only for backing up your wines and moving to a new phone.

What happens to my label photos?

The photo is sent to Claude, the AI that reads the label, and then dropped. Vinterest doesn't keep the image, and no photo is stored with your wines.

Where can I use it?

You can try the web app now. iPhone and Android apps are being prepared, and beta testers get them first.

How does the match work?

It predicts the score you'd give a wine from the wines you've already scored, weighting the ones most like it: same grape, region, producer and style. Under three scored wines of a type it says it's too early to call, rather than guess.
