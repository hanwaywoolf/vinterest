Scan

Every wine has a story.

Point your camera at any label or wine list$. The match comes back in moments. Then the bottle's story plays: about thirty seconds on who made it, how it will taste and whether it's for you, ending on your rating.

  1. Who made it, and when to drink it

    The producer and vintage, and where this bottle is in its life: young, at its best, or past it.

  2. Whether it's for you

    A match built from your WineDNA and your rating history, with one reason for and one against.

  3. How it will taste

    Body, tannins and acidity in everyday words, with what each one means, then the flavours to look for.

  4. The grape and the region

    The main grape, sketched, then where it's from on the map and what the climate does to the wine.

  5. Something to say about it

    One line you can say at the table to sound clued-in.

  6. Then rate it

    The story ends on the dial. Turn it, or skip when you're only checking the match.

  7. Save it, and go deeper

    It goes into My Wines and shapes your WineDNA. Add a few details, then see the full result or finish.

See the result and learn more

Go deeper on the bottle, in nine cards.

Once you've rated it, “See the result and learn more” opens a deck written for this exact bottle while you sip. It adds to the story rather than repeating it.

  1. Your match, in full

    The story gave you the match. The first card gives every reason behind it, in your own ratings.

  2. The house and the year

    Who makes it and what they're known for, then what this vintage was like and when to drink it.

  3. How it's made, and the region

    The grapes, the winemaking and what the label's words promise, then the region beyond the scan story's map.

  4. At the table, and while you taste

    How to serve it and three dishes that suit it, then cues for body, tannins and acidity as you sip.

  5. Test your palate with Blind Call

    Call its body, acidity and tannins before you look, lock it in, and see how close you were. The closer you are, the more XP you earn.

  6. Next from here

    The quizzes and reading this bottle opened, picked for you.

WineDNA

Every bottle builds your WineDNA.

Your ratings add up to a profile of the grapes, regions, styles and prices you love.

  1. A profile for each kind of wine

    Reds, whites, rosé and sparkling each get their own read. This drinker's reds come out Classic & Balanced, and the full profile in words is one tap away.

  2. What to buy next time

    Three rules written from your own ratings: reach for the grape or region that lifts them most, skip what holds them back, and try one new style.

  3. Your taste, visualized

    Where the wines you choose sit on each scale, from light to full, soft to grippy, with what each word means and how to notice it in the glass.

  4. Your sommelier script, in short

    A few lines to say to a sommelier or a shopkeeper, written from your WineDNA: what you like, in words they can act on.

  5. And the long version

    One tap gives the fuller script for when there's time to talk: the styles, the regions, what to steer clear of and what you'd like to try.

  6. What you'd buy again, and where to look next

    Your “house” wines, the bottles you keep coming back to, then your sweet spot on price and styles just outside your comfort zone.

  7. How well it knows you

    It re-predicts every rating you've given from your other bottles alone, and shows how close it got.

Mastery$

How well-rounded is your wine knowledge?

Mastery tracks what you actually know, across wine types, grapes, regions and your own palate. Reading and quizzes move your knowledge score, Blind Calls build your palate, and what you buy never counts.

  1. Your whole picture

    One score and a hand-drawn shape that shows where you're strong and where you're thin. A dotted outline shows where you were a month ago, so you can watch it grow.

  2. Grapes and regions

    Every grape is a berry on a sketched bunch that fills in as you study it. The map pins up as you learn a region and fades when your answers go stale. Tap either for its own page.

  3. Your palate, developing

    Play Blind Call on a bottle you're tasting and your guesses are checked against the label's profile. A tile for each trait shows how close you come, and any habit you have, like calling tannins grippier.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free?

You can start for free. Some features, like scanning whole wine lists and the Mastery map, are part of Vinterest Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wines and ratings live on your phone. An account is optional, and only for backing up your wines and moving to a new phone. You can also save a backup file and restore it on a new phone.

Do you store my label photos?

No. The photo is only used to read the label, then it's discarded. Vinterest doesn't keep the image, and no photo is saved with your wines.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, iPad and Android: get it from the App Store or Google Play. vinterest.app/get takes you to the right one for your phone.

How does the match work?

It predicts the rating you'd give a wine from the wines you've already rated, weighting the ones most like it: same grape, region, producer and style. The percentage is how sure it is that you'd enjoy it, so 90% means it's 90% sure. Under three rated wines of a type it says it's too early to call, rather than guess.
