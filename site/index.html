Scan

Scan a bottle. Know if it's for you.

Point your camera at any label or wine list$. You get a match score built from your own ratings, the reasons behind it, and your WineDNA.

  1. A match score you can easily understand

    A match score is a rating we expect you to give a wine. The more bottles you scan, the more developed your WineDNA and the more accurate your match score becomes.

  2. Reasons for and against, and exactly why

    Every match score comes with its reasons for and against, drawn from your WineDNA: the grapes, the regions, the producers you've rated before, and the styles you rate highest.

Learn about it

Learn about the wine, in nine cards.

One tap on “Learn about it” opens a deck written for this exact bottle.

  1. Start with your match score

    The first card is the match score and its rationale.

  2. Why you'll like it, and what to watch for

  3. Learn the origin story

Rate it

Taste it, then rate it out of 100.

Rating your wine is the fastest way to sharpen your next match score.

  1. Slide to rate

    Drag the slider or tap 70, 80, 85, 90 or 95. The label follows: 90 to 95 is Outstanding, 80 to 89 Very good.

  2. It feeds your WineDNA

    Save it and the bottle goes into My Wines and starts shaping your WineDNA and your next matches.

  3. Add some additional details, if you want

    Would you buy it again? What did you pay, and where did you have it? Your answers feed your Buy again list and your usual spend, and the place is searchable in My Wines.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free?

You can start for free. Some features, like scanning whole wine lists and the Mastery map, are part of Vinterest Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wines and ratings live on your phone. An account is optional, and only for backing up your wines and moving to a new phone.

What happens to my label photos?

The photo is sent to Claude, the AI that reads the label, and then dropped. Vinterest doesn't keep the image, and no photo is stored with your wines.

Where can I use it?

You can try the web app now. iPhone and Android apps are being prepared, and beta testers get them first.

How does the match work?

It predicts the rating you'd give a wine from the wines you've already rated, weighting the ones most like it: same grape, region, producer and style. Under three rated wines of a type it says it's too early to call, rather than guess.
