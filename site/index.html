Scan

Scan a bottle. Get its story.

Point your camera at any label or wine list$. In about thirty seconds Vinterest shows you what it found: who made it, how it will taste, and whether it's for you.

  1. Who made it, and when to drink it

    The producer and vintage, and where this bottle is in its life: young, at its best, or past it.

  2. Whether it's for you

    A match score built from your WineDNA and your rating history, with one reason for and one against.

  3. How it will taste

    Body, tannins and acidity in everyday words, with what each one means, then the flavours to look for.

  4. The grape

    The main grape, sketched, with what to notice about its skin and its bunch.

  5. The region

    Where it's from on the map, and what the climate does to the wine.

  6. Something to say about it

    One line you can say at the table to sound clued-in.

  7. Then rate it, or see more

    Rate it, learn more about the bottle, save it for later or open the full result.

Rate it

Rate it, then keep learning.

Rating your wine is the fastest way to sharpen your next match score. Then Vinterest picks what to learn from the bottle you just had.

  1. Slide to rate

    Drag the slider or tap 70, 80, 85, 90 or 95. The label follows: 90 to 95 is Outstanding, 80 to 89 Very good.

  2. Save it, and add details if you like

    The bottle goes into My Wines and starts shaping your WineDNA. You can add whether you'd buy it again, what you paid and where you had it.

  3. Then keep learning

    Quizzes and articles picked for the bottle you just had: its wine type, its grape and its region.

WineDNA

Every bottle builds your WineDNA.

Your ratings add up to a profile of the grapes, regions, styles and prices you love.

  1. A profile for each kind of wine

    Reds, whites, rosé and sparkling each get their own read. This drinker's reds come out Classic & Balanced.

  2. What you love, in plain words

    Against your own usual score: the style, flavours, grapes and regions you rate highest, and the bottles that prove it.

  3. How well it knows you

    It re-predicts your scores to show how close it got, then lists your “house” wines: the bottles you keep coming back to.

  4. Your taste, visualized

    The traits you choose, visualized, with what each word means and how to notice it.

  5. Value, and where to look next

    Your sweet spot and the bottles that beat their price, then styles just outside your comfort zone.

  6. Your sommelier script

    A short, quick version or a longer, more detailed one when you need it: lines to say to a restaurant sommelier, written from your WineDNA.

Mastery$

How well-rounded is your wine knowledge?

Mastery tracks what you actually know, across wine types, grapes, regions and your own palate. Reading and quizzes move your knowledge score, Blind Calls build your palate, and what you buy never counts.

  1. Your whole picture

    One score and a hand-drawn shape that shows where you're strong and where you're thin. A dotted outline shows where you were a month ago, so you can watch it grow.

  2. Grapes and regions

    Every grape is a berry on a sketched bunch that fills in as you study it. The map pins up as you learn a region and fades when your answers go stale. Tap either for its own page.

  3. Your palate, developing

    Play Blind Call on a bottle you're tasting and your guesses are checked against the label's profile. A tile for each trait shows how close you come, and any habit you have, like calling tannins grippier.

Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free?

You can start for free. Some features, like scanning whole wine lists and the Mastery map, are part of Vinterest Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. Your wines and ratings live on your phone. An account is optional, and only for backing up your wines and moving to a new phone. You can also save a backup file and restore it on a new phone.

Do you store my label photos?

No. The photo is only used to read the label, then it's discarded. Vinterest doesn't keep the image, and no photo is saved with your wines.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, iPad and Android. The apps are being prepared, and beta testers get them first.

How does the match work?

It predicts the rating you'd give a wine from the wines you've already rated, weighting the ones most like it: same grape, region, producer and style. Under three rated wines of a type it says it's too early to call, rather than guess.
