Privacy notice

Last updated 18 October 2026

Vinterest helps you learn what you like in wine. This notice says what we collect, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. It covers the Vinterest app for iPhone and Android and this website.

Who we are

Vinterest ("we", "us") makes the app and this website. You can reach us at hello@vinterest.app for anything in this notice.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. Your wines, ratings and progress live on your phone.
  • If you sign in, we keep a backup of them so they follow you to a new phone.
  • Label photos are read by an AI service and not kept.
  • We don't run ads, don't sell your data, and don't track you across other apps or sites.
  • You can delete your account and everything we hold for it from inside the app.

What stays on your phone

Your scanned wines, ratings, notes, what you paid, where you had a wine, favourites, quiz answers, reading progress, XP and settings are stored on your device. Without an account, none of it reaches us. Deleting the app removes it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. You give your email address and we send a six-digit code to it. We keep your email address and a copy of your wines, XP, settings and learning progress, so a backup can restore them on a new phone. The copy is tied to your account and only your account can read it. If you used a promo code, we record that your account has Pro and until when.

What is sent to an AI service

Parts of the app use Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, and requests to it go through our own server.

  • When you scan a label or a wine list, the photo is sent to Claude to be read. We don't keep the photo and it isn't saved with your wines. What it reads (the wine's name, grapes, region and so on) is saved with the wine.
  • When you ask Vinny a question, open an article written for you, or ask for a tasting summary, quiz or sommelier script, the request includes a short summary of your taste: your top grapes and regions, your ratings, recent bottles and what you usually spend. It doesn't include your name or email address.
  • To show a shop price, the name of a wine (never who you are) may be looked up on the web.

Anthropic processes these requests to give us the answer. We don't use them to build a profile of you, and we keep no log of the content on our server.

Where you are

You tell the app your country, and sometimes a province and a city, so it can show prices in your currency and the right shops. You type these in; the app doesn't read your phone's GPS location. Your choices are stored on your device, and in your backup if you sign in. The camera is used only while you scan, and the photo library only for a photo you choose.

Notifications

If you switch on push notifications in the app and you're signed in, we store a token that Apple or Google gives your phone so we can reach it, with your phone's time zone and the app version. We use it to send reminders about your own wines (a bottle waiting for a rating, a long gap since your last scan), a note when promotional Pro access is about to end, and a note if a subscription payment fails. We send at most one a day, around 6pm where you are, and you can turn them off in the app at any time, or in your phone's settings. Turning them off, signing out or deleting your account removes the token. The notification text is sent through Apple's and Google's push services.

Shops and partner links

Some links to wine shops are partner links, marked "Partner" in the app. If you buy through one, the shop may pay us a commission at no extra cost to you. Clicks go through our server and then an affiliate network (Awin, or Skimlinks), which records the click and the sale with a cookie on the shop's site so the shop knows the sale came from Vinterest. We don't send the network or the shop your name, email or wines. Partners never change a match, a rating or what Vinterest suggests.

Age

The app asks you to confirm you are of legal drinking age where you live. It isn't for anyone under it, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

This website

The website doesn't set cookies. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits (which pages, roughly where from, what kind of device). It doesn't use cookies or follow you across other sites. There is no analytics or advertising tracking inside the app.

Joining the beta

If you join the beta list on the website, we collect your first name, last name, country and email address. We use them to invite you and to email you about the beta. We send a short confirmation email when you sign up. We don't sell the list or use it for anything else.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and our server, and provides the website analytics.
  • Supabase stores account backups and the beta list.
  • Anthropic processes label photos and questions, as described above.
  • Awin and Skimlinks track clicks on partner shop links, as described above.
  • Resend sends the beta confirmation email.
  • Apple and Google run the App Store and Google Play, and deliver push notifications through their own services. They tell us about purchases and crashes under their own policies.

These companies may process data outside your own country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, they do so under standard contractual protections.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete your account. Beta list details stay until the beta ends or you ask us to remove them. Our server keeps short-lived technical records, such as your network address, only to limit abuse and fix faults.

Your choices and rights

In the app, "Delete account" on your account card removes your account and everything we hold for it, and you can save a backup file first. You can also sign out at any time and keep using the app with the data on your phone.

Depending on where you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to give you a copy, or to stop using it. Email hello@vinterest.app and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Connections are encrypted, accounts are protected by emailed codes, and each account can read only its own rows. No system is perfectly safe, so we keep what we hold to what the app needs.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page and, for beta members, by email. The date at the top shows the latest version.
