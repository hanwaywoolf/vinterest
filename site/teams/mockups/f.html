How it works

A photo of the list. Five minutes a shift. One number per person.

01 · SEND THE LIST

Photograph the list and the menu.

We read them and write a card for every wine and a pairing for every dish, from checked facts about the region and grape. Nothing reaches staff until you've seen what changed and said yes. A new bottle joins from its label in under a minute.

02 · FIVE A SHIFT

Each person gets five questions before service.

The wines they sell most, the dish a guest just ordered, the bottle easiest to confuse with the one next to it. They say how sure they are first; what they get wrong comes back sooner, what they know comes back later.

03 · SEE WHO'S READY

One figure per person, against the standard you set.

Ready, slipping, or not yet, for their role. Who's overdue, which new wines nobody knows, which glass pours too few people can sell. Checked without warning, so it's honest.

Fair questions

What owners ask before they say yes.

Our list changes every week.

Send the new one, or photograph the new bottle. The review shows what's new, gone and changed; a new vintage is the same wine moving on, so nobody relearns it. The training follows the list the same day.

My staff won't do homework.

It's five questions before service, on the phone they're already holding, about tonight's wines and dishes. Nobody is asked to study.

Will it write nonsense about my wines?

Every card comes from checked facts about the region and grape, written against your list and menu, and you read it before anyone else does. Rewrite any line in your own words and that's what they learn.

What about our till?

A sales export from any till works today and tells the console what sells and who sells it, so what moves most is learnt first. A Square connector comes next. Sales never change anyone's readiness.

What does it cost?

Per site, per month, any number of staff, with tiers by the size of the team and the list and the rigour you want. We're in pilot now at a pilot rate; accounts and billing per site come next. Groups by conversation.

Where does our list go?

Your list and menu are read once to write the training, which stays with your restaurant. No guest data is held, and nothing is sold to anyone else. The same standard applies whether you're one pub or a group.
