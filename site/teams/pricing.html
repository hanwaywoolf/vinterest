There's more to software than switching it on.

Some tools have you live in an afternoon: load your list and you're set. A list that's only been loaded teaches what the label says. Yours is built by our team, with yours, before anyone on the floor answers a question.

Fair questions

About the money.

What does a pilot cost?

Half the monthly price for your first 90 days, with mise en place included: about £65 a month for a restaurant on the month-to-month price. There's no term to sign until you've seen the 90-day review.

What is the mise en place fee for?

Our team's work before go-live: reading in your list and menu and checking them against the originals, the analysis and recommendations, the pairings, the training and its reviews, and the targets in your plan. It's charged once per site and included in a pilot.

Why not just upload the list and go?

You can load a list in minutes, and after go-live your owner publishes changes that way. The first build is different: a wrong vintage, a glass price read as a bottle or a pairing nobody checked gets drilled into the whole team, so we check it with you first.

Does changing the list or running specials cost more?

No. List and menu changes, nightly specials, 86'd dishes and plans for individual staff are in every plan.

Which currencies can I pay in?

Pick your currency at the top of the page: pounds, US, Canadian, Australian or New Zealand dollars, or euros. Each market has its own set prices, so they don't move with the exchange rate. Anywhere else, ask us and we'll quote yours.

How would I know it paid for itself?

Bring in your POS sales before and during. The console shows wine sales beside what the team knows, so you can judge the lift yourself rather than take ours.
