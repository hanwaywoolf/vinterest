Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 7 November 2026. What we collect on vinterest.team and in the Vinterest Teams pilot, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it.

Who we are

Vinterest ("we", "us") makes Vinterest Teams, which trains a restaurant's staff on its own wine list and menu, and this website. You can reach us at hello@vinterest.app for anything in this notice. Vinterest for wine drinkers has its own notice at vinterest.app/privacy.

The short version

  • The website doesn't set cookies or track you across other sites.
  • If you sign up for a pilot, we keep what you tell us in the form and use it only to set up and run your pilot.
  • We don't run ads and we don't sell or share your details.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete what we hold at any time.

Signing up for a pilot

The pilot form asks for your first and last name, work email, business name, city and country, the type of business, how many locations and front-of-house staff it has, its POS system, roughly how many wines are on its list, and, if you choose to add them, your role and any notes.

We use these details to reply to you, to decide with you whether a pilot fits, and to set it up: the size of the list and the team tell us how much training to write, and the POS tells us how your sales can come in. When you send the form, we email you a confirmation and email the request to our own team, so we can reply to you directly. We don't add you to a mailing list or use your details for anything else.

We use them because you asked us to talk to you about a pilot (where the law asks for a basis, that's taking steps at your request before an agreement, and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries).

This website

The website doesn't set cookies. We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits (which pages, roughly where from, what kind of device); it doesn't use cookies or follow you across other sites. To fill in the country on the form for you, the site asks Cloudflare which country your connection comes from; it isn't stored. Our server keeps your network address for a few minutes, in memory only, to stop the form being sent over and over by a script.

"Try Restaurant Demo" opens The Larkspur, an invented restaurant. What you do in the demo stays in your own browser, apart from what you ask Vinny or say to a guest at the table, which is answered through our server as described below.

The Teams app during the pilot

During the pilot, a restaurant's wine list, menu, training, staff names and roles, everyone's progress and any sales data it imports are kept on the restaurant's own devices, not on our servers.

Parts of the app use Claude, an AI service from Anthropic, through our own server: reading photos of the list and menu, writing the training cards and pairings, answering questions put to Vinny, and playing a guest at the table. These requests carry the wine list, the menu and the question or reply, not a staff member's name or email. If Voice is on, the guest's lines are spoken by ElevenLabs (or OpenAI), and a spoken reply is sent to ElevenLabs to be written down; the recording isn't kept by us or saved with anything. How someone sounded (pace, fillers) is worked out on the device and shown only to them.

Who else handles your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and our server, and provides the website analytics.
  • Supabase stores pilot requests.
  • Resend sends the confirmation email and passes the request to our team.
  • Anthropic processes the requests from the Teams app described above.
  • ElevenLabs and OpenAI turn text into speech, and ElevenLabs turns speech into text, when Voice is on in the Teams app.

These companies may process data outside your own country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, they do so under standard contractual protections.

How long we keep it

We keep a pilot request while we're talking to you about a pilot and for as long as the pilot runs. If it doesn't go ahead, we delete the request within twelve months, or sooner if you ask. Emails we exchange are kept as ordinary business correspondence.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to give you a copy, or to stop using it. Email hello@vinterest.app and we'll reply within a month. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner).

Security

Connections are encrypted, and pilot requests can be read only with our server's key, never from a browser. No system is perfectly safe, so we keep what we hold to what a pilot needs.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page, and email anyone with a pilot request open. The date at the top shows the latest version.
