A menu that changes daily

Today's menu, paired before service.

Your menu changes every day and your list holds 313 wines, so no printed pairing sheet lasts the week. Vinterest Teams is built for that.

1

Send today's menu

A photo or a PDF of the day's menu. New dishes are read and paired from your own list: the three best wines for each, a pick by the glass and the one to steer away from. Dishes that carry over keep what they had.

2

You approve it

Nothing reaches the team until a manager has read it. Swap a wine, change the line, and confirm the kitchen's allergens for each new dish.

3

Everyone reads it before the floor

The pre-shift briefing opens over the app: what's new, what's 86'd, what to pour with it. Each person accepts it before they start, and you see who has.
