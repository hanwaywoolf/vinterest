Before you say yes.

Our list changes every week.

Good. Upload the new one, or photograph the new bottle, and Vinny has the training ready the same day. A new vintage is the same wine moving on; nobody relearns it.

My staff won't do homework.

It isn't homework. It's five questions before service, on the phone they're already holding, about tonight's wines and dishes. Someone new starts with the glass list and works up in waves, not the whole list on day one.

Will it write nonsense about my wines?

Every card is grounded in checked facts about the region and the grape, written against your list, and you read it before anyone else does. Rewrite any line in your own words.

What about our POS?

Integrate your POS or import a sales CSV from any POS. It tells us which wines sell and who on the team serves them, so what matters most is learnt first. A CSV works with any POS today, and Square is the first direct connection we're building. Sales never change anyone's readiness.

How do you know the questions are right?

Every question goes through three checks before anyone sees it: rules that catch giveaways and wrong answers that could be right, a read as the drill shows it, and a review as a head sommelier would give it. A question still in doubt is rewritten or held back. The console shows any question nearly everyone gets right first time, or most people miss.

What about allergens?

The app drafts each dish's allergens from the menu, and nobody is asked about or shown one until the kitchen confirms it in the console. Then every role needs every confirmed dish at 100%, and you can export who has passed as a CSV.

What does it cost?

Per site, per month, any number of staff, with tiers by the size of the list and the training level you want. Pilot rates for the first restaurants; groups by conversation.

Where does our list go?

It's read once to write your training, which stays with your restaurant. No guest data, nothing sold on. The same standard for one restaurant or a group of forty.
