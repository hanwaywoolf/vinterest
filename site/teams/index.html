Fair questions

Before you say yes.

Our list changes every week.

Good. Send the new one, or photograph the new bottle, and the training follows the same day. A new vintage is the same wine moving on; nobody relearns it.

My staff won't do homework.

It isn't homework. It's a few minutes before service, on the phone they're already holding, about tonight's wines and dishes.

Will it write nonsense about my wines?

Every card is grounded in checked facts about the region and the grape, written against your list, and you read it before anyone else does. Rewrite any line in your own words.

What about our till?

A sales export from any till works today. It tells us what sells and who sells it, so what matters most is learnt first. Square is next. Sales never change anyone's readiness.

What does it cost?

Per site, per month, any number of staff, with tiers by the size of the team and the list and the standard you want. Pilot rates for the first restaurants; groups by conversation.

Where does our list go?

It's read once to write your training, which stays with your restaurant. No guest data, nothing sold on. The same standard for one pub or a group of forty.
