Getting started

Mise en place. Everything in its place before your first service.

Kitchens call the prep before the doors open mise en place: everything ready, so service runs itself. That's how we launch Vinterest Teams. We do it with you, in about two weeks from the first call to day one, and we stay close for the 90 days after.

1Week 1

Discovery

A call with your team and ours to learn how you run, so the plan fits your business and your team is ready on day one.

  • What you use now: your POS, cellar management, reservations and any other systems.
  • Your team: who's on the floor, behind the bar and on the wine, and how often people join.
  • Your guests: who comes in, what they order and what they ask about.
  • How you train today: what works, what doesn't, and how long it takes someone new.
  • Your list and menu: how deep they go and how often they change.
  • Your numbers: current wine sales and the targets you're working to.
  • Your baseline: a recent sales export, so wine per guest and glass and bottle sales are recorded before launch and there's a real "before" to measure against.
  • What success means for you: agreed up front, such as readiness by role, wine per guest or bottle sales, and written into the plan.

Anything custom you need comes up here too, so it's in the plan from the start.

2Week 2

Your launch plan

We write a plan that fits your business, so you're ready to use Vinterest Teams on day one. You see it and agree it before anything goes live.

  • Integrations: a sales export from any POS works today, and Square is the first direct connection we're building. Anything else, such as cellar management, is scoped with you.
  • Your list and menu: loaded from your files or photos, with a card for every wine, pairings for every dish and notes on every plate.
  • Training by role: the level for each role (Low, Medium or High) and the minimum each needs to be ready.
  • Your team, now and later: current staff set up, and new starters taken through the list in waves rather than all at once.
  • Testing and checks: every question checked by the app's own review and a sommelier review before your team sees it.
  • Reporting and ROI: what the console will show you, and how it's measured against your baseline.
  • Ownership: a named owner on your side who runs it day to day, and who else can open the console.
  • Data and privacy: what's held, where it's kept, and how voice practice works for your team.
  • Timeline and support: the go-live date and who to call along the way.
3End of week 2

Go-live

We launch before a service you choose, never your busiest night, with everything from the plan in place.

  • Your team on their phones: a 20-minute session gets front of house set up and through their first drill together.
  • Your managers on the console: readiness, Tonight, the menu and allergens, and how to publish a list change.
  • Allergens signed off: the kitchen confirms each dish's allergens first, since staff are never shown one the kitchen hasn't confirmed.
  • Tonight set: what's 86'd and what's on feature, on every phone from the first service.
  • Watched closely: for the first two weeks we look at usage every day and fix anything that gets in the way the same day.
  • A check-in at the end of week one: with your owner, on what's working and what isn't.
4Days 1 to 90

The first 90 days

We work with your management team to review how it's being used at 30, 60 and 90 days, and improve it with what your team tells us.

  • Measured against your baseline: readiness by role, and wine per guest and glass and bottle sales against where you started.
  • What your team says: feedback from the floor, turned into changes.
  • More when you're ready: not every restaurant wants everything on day one. We add the rest as your team is ready for it, such as a deeper training level, Vinny's guest at the table, voice practice or Sales Insights once your POS is in, without overwhelming anyone in the first few months.
5Ongoing

Built for you

As we work together, some things will help your team that are unique to you. We capture them, scope them with you and quote them before we build, so they close the gap and add value for your team and your guests.

After 90 days

Still there when you need us.

A named contact on our side, regular reviews with your owner, and list changes handled the way you prefer: your owner publishes them, or we do it for you.

Restaurant groups

One site first, then the rest.

We launch at a pilot site, learn from it, then roll out to the others with the same minimums, each site on its own list and menu.

Your data
