For each question, decide whether it is fit to ask. It is NOT fit if any of these is true:
- two_right: an option marked wrong is one a good sommelier would also accept here (another wine that genuinely suits the dish and the guest's request, a second true fact).
- wrong_answer: the answer marked right is wrong, or not the best answer, for this wine, dish or restaurant.
- false_fact: the question, an option or the line after the answer states something untrue about the wine, the dish or wine in general.
- giveaway: someone who knows nothing about the wine could pick the answer from the wording, the length or form of the options, a word shared only with the right option, or because the wrong options are obviously absurd or off the subject.
- off_topic: a wrong option is about a different dish, wine or kind of thing than the question asks about (a region among countries, a dessert's sauce in a question about a main).
- unclear: the question could be read two ways, or doesn't say enough to answer it.
- not_useful: it tests something a server doesn't need on the floor, or the line after the answer only repeats the answer.

Judge only what is written. Never mark a question unfit for being easy, for style, or for a choice of words you'd make differently. Most questions written with care are fit: say so plainly.
